    Seasonal pattern

    Typical year: median of each calendar month over all other years (band = middle 50 %), compared with the latest year. Shows when the fire season is.

    Years compared

    Total per year with a linear trend over complete years. Hatched = mostly converted from MODIS, light = incomplete year.

    Harmonization check

    Raw MODIS and VIIRS, MODIS converted with the calibration factor, and the harmonized series with its uncertainty band. Converted MODIS should run close to VIIRS where both exist.

    Cross-check with the data team (Tabelle B)

    Grid

    What this is: a control that FireCal and the data team compute the same numbers. The data team delivers sums per grid cell and fixed time window (“Tabelle B”). FireCal recomputes the same sums from the single hotspots, for the area selected on the map, without low confidence (like the team table). Green = equal within 0.5 %.
    Time span: the windows of the team table (shown in the first column), not the timeline selection. FRP = sum of the fire radiative power of all detections in that window (MW); “per day” divides by the days of the window. Team cells count when their centre is inside the area, so small differences at the edge of a drawn area are expected.

    Fire intensity (FRP)

    Share of detections per FRP class (fire radiative power of one detection, MW). VIIRS sees many small fires that MODIS misses.

    Period –

    Local time of detection

    Satellites pass at fixed local times (Terra ~10:30 / 22:30, Aqua and VIIRS ~13:30 / 01:30). Peaks are overpasses, not fire behaviour.

    Confidence

    Share of low / nominal / high per sensor. Click “low” to include or exclude it everywhere.

    Satellites

    Detections per satellite. Click a bar to switch that satellite on or off (raw values and map).

    Satellite timeline

    Raw detections per satellite and period (log colour). New satellites (NOAA-20 2018, NOAA-21 2023; S-NPP 2012 in older data) raise raw counts without more fire, which is why raw counts are not comparable over time. The orange frame is the selected period.

    Calibration factors (MODIS → VIIRS)

    Factor = VIIRS / MODIS in cells where both detect fire. The band (25–75 %) is the uncertainty used above. The region of the selected area is highlighted.
